About

A tester who also builds

I finished my Diploma in Computer Science at Acharya Institute of Technology (2023–2026). Since then I've built and deployed two full-stack projects: FreshCart, a grocery store in Java and Spring Boot, and PortfolioHub, a portfolio builder in React and Firebase.

My habit is to try to break what I build. For FreshCart I compared the checkout with apps like Swiggy Instamart, listed every edge case they handle, then wrote code and a JUnit test for each one: two people buying the last item, double-clicking "Place order", prices changing mid-checkout.

I'm looking for a QA or Software Tester intern or fresher role where I can find bugs early and help the team ship with confidence.
